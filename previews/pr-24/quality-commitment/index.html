Quality Commitment

Care for every customer

As a family-owned business, we work directly with your team on special requests and extra deliveries. We set aside and return guest belongings and hotel items we find mixed in with the laundry.

For urgent requests, call our team at 847-593-8888.

Expert cleaning

Some of our employees have been with RKC for 10 to 15 years. Their hands-on experience shapes how we handle hotel linens and guest garments.

Commercial washing equipment

We use a tunnel washer to handle the volume of linens and towels hotels send us for cleaning.

Cleaning products and quality checks

We use high-quality laundry cleaning products from suppliers such as Ecolab. Before packing, our team checks linens for stains and damage and sets aside items that need another wash.

Water recycling

Our laundry facility uses a water recycling system so we can reuse water during laundering.
