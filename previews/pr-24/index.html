Quality Commitment

Care in every step.

01 / Pickup

At your hotel

We pick up linens and guest garments at your hotel. Contact our team to schedule a pickup or arrange an extra delivery. Need help right away? Call 847-593-8888.

02 / Clean

Care in the wash

We clean hotel linens and towels using state-of-the-art laundry equipment and high-quality cleaning products.

03 / Pack

Attention to detail

Some of our team members have been with RKC for 10 to 15 years. They bring that experience and care to every order, checking linens for stains and damage, setting aside items that need another wash, and labeling each order by hotel.

04 / Deliver

Back to your team

We have a team to deliver the cleaned laundry to the right hotel on time. They check each order’s property label before delivery and return any guest belongings or hotel items we find in the laundry.
