Frequently asked questions

How do pickup and delivery work?
Our team picks up laundry at your hotel and returns it clean and packed. Contact us to discuss a recurring schedule and confirm turnaround for your property.
Can I request an extra delivery?
Yes. Contact our team to arrange an extra delivery. For urgent requests, call 847-593-8888.
Can you help if our usual laundry service is interrupted?
Yes. We can help new customers with temporary or emergency laundry service when we have availability. Call 847-593-8888 to tell us what you need and when.
